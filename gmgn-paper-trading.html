What Is GMGN Paper Trading?

Paper trading allows traders to practice with simulated funds instead of real capital.

Simora adds paper trading tools to supported GMGN pages so traders can practice interacting with fast-moving memecoin markets without executing real blockchain transactions.

How to Paper Trade on GMGN

1

Install Simora

Add the free Simora extension from the Chrome Web Store.

2

Open GMGN

Visit a supported token, trading or token-listing page on GMGN.

3

Start Paper Trading

Use Simora to simulate entries and exits, track positions and monitor paper trading PnL.

No wallet connection, activation key or payment is required.

Practice GMGN Trading Without Real Funds

Simora uses simulated trades that do not move assets or execute blockchain transactions.

Traders can practice:

  • Entries and exits
  • Position sizing
  • Fast execution practice
  • PnL management
  • New token evaluation practice
  • Reviewing past performance

Paper trading cannot reproduce every condition of live trading and does not guarantee future results.
