Frequently Asked Questions

Is Simora free?

Yes. Simora is 100% free to use. There are no activation keys, subscriptions, paid plans, or locked premium features.

Which platforms does Simora support?

Simora supports:

  • Axiom
  • GMGN
  • Padre

Do I need to connect a wallet?

No. Simora is a paper trading extension and does not require a wallet connection. No private keys or real funds are used for simulated trades.

My trading data disappeared

Simora stores trading data locally in your browser. Data may be lost if:

  • Browser or extension storage is cleared
  • The extension is uninstalled
  • Browser data is reset

Simora isn't working on my trading platform

Make sure Simora is enabled and that you are using a supported trading page.

Troubleshooting steps:

  • Refresh the trading page
  • Check that Simora is enabled in Chrome
  • Make sure you are on a supported token or trading page
  • Temporarily disable other extensions if you suspect a conflict

Is my trading data private?

Simulated trading data is stored locally in your browser. Simora does not require access to your wallet or private keys.

Installation Issues

Simora isn't loading

  1. Make sure you are using a Chromium-based browser supported by the extension.
  2. Check that Simora is enabled in your browser extensions.
  3. Refresh the trading platform page.
  4. Restart the browser if the issue continues.
  5. Contact support if the problem persists.

Performance Issues

Prices or trading data are not updating

  • Check your internet connection
  • Refresh the trading page
  • Make sure you are on a supported token or trading page
  • Temporarily disable other extensions if necessary
  • Contact support if the issue continues
