Getting Started

  • Each Weekly Challenge starts with a 10 SOL virtual balance.
  • The Join window is open only during the first two days.
  • Trading is Solana only across supported Simora platforms.
  • Your Challenge balance and progress follow you across supported platforms.

Trading

  • Only realized cash counts toward the final result.
  • Open positions at the deadline do not count toward the final score.
  • Positions must be closed before the deadline for their result to count.
  • Reset Simulator is disabled while the Challenge is active.
  • Your normal simulator portfolio remains preserved separately.

Devices & Leaving

  • Only one device can actively control a Challenge at a time.
  • Take Ownership transfers Challenge control to another device.
  • Leaving is final for that week; you cannot rejoin the same Challenge.

Final Results

  • Rankings and results are finalized after the Challenge ends.
  • No rewards or prizes are guaranteed.
  • Technical corrections may be made when necessary.
