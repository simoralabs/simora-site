Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Simora is a free memecoin paper trading extension for Axiom, GMGN and Padre. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the extension.

Information We Collect

Local Data

  • Trading data: Paper trading positions, transactions and performance data used by Simora.
  • Settings: Preferences and configuration settings.
  • Cache data: Temporary market and price data required for extension functionality.

How We Use Information

Local Functionality

  • Store and manage your paper trading data.
  • Provide real-time paper trading simulation.
  • Calculate PnL and trading performance.
  • Provide performance history and analytics.
  • Save extension preferences and settings.

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • Paper trading data is primarily stored locally in your browser.
  • Local extension settings and preferences are stored in browser storage.
  • Users remain in control of locally stored Simora data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

We do not use users' wallets, private keys, or real trading funds.

Your Data

You can remove locally stored Simora data by clearing the extension's storage or uninstalling the extension.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact: simoralabs@gmail.com

Third-Party Services

Simora interacts with supported trading platforms in order to provide its functionality, including:

  • Axiom
  • GMGN
  • Padre

These services have their own privacy policies and practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Simora's features or data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
