Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Simora is a free memecoin paper trading extension for Axiom, GMGN and Padre. It simulates trades using live market data. No real funds, wallets or blockchain transactions are involved.

Simora works inside your own browser. Your paper trading account, positions, history and settings are stored on your device, and Simora does not send them to its own servers or to anyone else. This policy explains what is kept on your device, the few requests the extension makes to outside services, and who receives them.

Information Stored on Your Device

The extension creates and stores the following information in your browser. It stays on your device:

  • Paper trading account data: your simulated balance, open positions and per-token accounting records, kept separately for each supported chain (SOL, BNB, Robinhood).
  • Simulated transactions: your paper buy and sell history, including amounts, prices and timestamps.
  • Performance data: calculated profit and loss, realized results and related statistics.
  • Pending limit orders created inside the simulator.
  • Share PnL backgrounds: an image, GIF or video you choose as a Share PnL card background. It is kept in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere. Cards you copy or download are created on your device.
  • Settings and preferences: display options, panel positions and sizes, sounds, slippage and fee settings, chart preferences and language settings.
  • Temporary cache data: recent market and price information needed to display live values, and cached chart markers.

Information Sent Outside Your Device

Simora has no account system and does not run servers that receive your data. The extension only makes the following requests:

  • Market prices: requests to Binance, with CoinGecko as a fallback, for the public USD prices of SOL, BNB and ETH. These requests contain no personal or account information.
  • Token information from the supported platforms: on Axiom and GMGN, the extension can request public token information (such as name, image and market data) for the token you are viewing from that platform's own servers, using the token's address. These requests go only to the platform you are using, the way the platform's own page makes them, and your browser may attach that platform's own cookies. Simora does not read or store those cookies.
  • Auto Translate: if you turn on Auto Translate, the text of the social posts it translates is sent to Google's public translation endpoint. Nothing else is sent, and Simora does not store that text.
  • Icon font: the extension loads an icon font from Google Fonts. Like any web request, this lets Google see your IP address; no other information is included.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Simora does not collect names, email addresses, usernames or any other information that identifies you.
  • Simora does not collect or request private keys, seed phrases or recovery phrases.
  • Simora does not connect to, control or use your cryptocurrency wallet.
  • Simora does not execute real blockchain transactions and does not move real funds.
  • Simora does not take custody of cryptocurrency or any other asset.
  • Simora does not collect your browsing history. The extension only runs on Axiom, GMGN and Padre.
  • Simora does not track your clicks or activity and does not use analytics.
  • Simora does not use your information for advertising or ad targeting.

How Information Is Used

  • Simulate paper trades using live market prices.
  • Calculate profit and loss, and update it in real time.
  • Display your open and closed positions and your trading history.
  • Maintain your performance history across sessions.
  • Remember your settings, preferences and interface layout.
  • Translate social posts when you turn on Auto Translate.

Data Storage and Retention

Your paper trading account, simulated transactions, performance history, limit orders, Share PnL backgrounds, settings and cached market data are stored using your browser's storage for the supported platforms (local storage and IndexedDB) and the extension's own storage. Simora does not upload these records and does not synchronize them itself.

A small number of preferences, such as panel visibility, language, Auto Translate and Quick Buy settings, are saved in the browser's synced extension storage. If you use your browser's own sync feature, your browser provider may sync those preferences between your devices. Your paper trading account, positions and trading history are not stored there.

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or clear your browser data. Uninstalling the extension removes the extension's own storage. Your paper trading account and history are kept in your browser's storage for the supported platforms, so they remain on your device after uninstalling until you clear the site data for Axiom, GMGN and Padre.

Retired Online Features

Earlier versions of Simora included two optional online features, Simora Social and the Weekly Challenge. Both have been retired. Since version 1.1.7, the extension no longer sends any information to Simora's servers, and when it updates it removes the Simora Social and Weekly Challenge data that earlier versions kept in your browser.

If you used these features in an earlier version, the information you chose to share then (such as your username, profile picture, shared trades and Weekly Challenge results) may still be held on Simora's backend, which runs on Cloudflare. Inactive Simora Social accounts are deleted automatically after 3 months. Final Weekly Challenge results, which include usernames, may remain viewable on our website as a record of past challenges.

To request deletion of this information sooner, contact simoralabs@gmail.com and include your username or public profile code.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not share personal data for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • Your paper trading data stays on your device and is not shared with Axiom, GMGN, Padre or anyone else.

The outside services listed above receive only the requests described in this policy. They operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Axiom, GMGN and Padre: the supported trading platforms Simora works on.
  • Binance and CoinGecko: public market prices.
  • Google: translation for Auto Translate, and the icon font.

Your Choices

Auto Translate is optional. It is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in the extension popup.

Deleting your data. Use Reset Simulator in the extension to clear your trade history. To remove everything, clear the site data for Axiom, GMGN and Padre in your browser and uninstall the extension.

Children

Simora is not directed to children and is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided information through one of Simora's retired online features, please contact us at simoralabs@gmail.com and we will remove it.
