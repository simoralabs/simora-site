Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Simora is a free memecoin paper trading extension for Axiom, GMGN and Padre. It simulates trades using live market data. No real funds, wallets or blockchain transactions are involved.

Most of what Simora does happens inside your own browser. Your paper trading account, positions, history and settings are stored on your device.

Simora also includes optional online features — Simora Social and the Weekly Challenge, including its leaderboards. These features do not work locally: when you choose to use them, certain information is sent to and stored on Simora's backend servers. This policy explains what is kept on your device, what is sent to Simora's servers, how it is used, and who else can see it.

Information We Collect

Data stored on your device

This information is created and stored locally by the extension, in your browser's own storage. It is not sent to Simora's servers.

  • Paper trading account data: your simulated balance, open positions, and per-token accounting records, kept separately for each supported chain (SOL, BNB, Robinhood).
  • Simulated transactions: your paper buy and sell history, including amounts, prices and timestamps.
  • Performance data: calculated profit and loss, realized results and related statistics.
  • Pending limit orders created inside the simulator.
  • Settings and preferences: display options, panel positions and sizes, sounds, slippage and fee settings, chart preferences, and language settings.
  • Temporary cache data: recent market and price information needed to display live values, and cached chart markers.

Data sent to Simora's servers (Simora Social and Weekly Challenge)

The following is processed by Simora's backend only if you create a Simora Social profile, sign in, or take part in the Weekly Challenge.

  • Profile information: your chosen username, an automatically generated public profile code, and your profile picture if you upload one.
  • Account credentials: if you create a password-protected account, the password you choose is sent to our servers when you register, sign in, or secure an existing profile.
  • Account and session identifiers: an account identifier assigned by our servers, and session tokens that keep you signed in. Sessions expire automatically.
  • An installation identifier: a random identifier generated by your browser when the extension first needs one. It is not a hardware or browser fingerprint and does not identify you personally.
  • Simora Social visibility setting: whether you have turned Simora Social on.
  • Profiles you follow: the public profile codes you add to your Social list.
  • Shared trade activity: when Simora Social is on, your completed paper trades are shared through our servers with people who follow you. Each shared trade includes the token name and contract address, the chain, whether it was a buy or sell, the simulated amount and token quantity, the market cap at the time, and the time of the trade.
  • Weekly Challenge participation: the challenge you joined, when you joined or left, and your participation status.
  • Weekly Challenge trades: each challenge trade is sent to our servers as an operation record containing the token contract address, whether it was a buy or sell, the simulated amount or the fraction sold, the market cap and the base asset price at execution, the maximum fee setting used, the time of execution, and which supported platform (Axiom, GMGN or Padre) it was made on.
  • Weekly Challenge results: your challenge balance, realized performance, number of trades, win rate and ranking, which our servers calculate from your submitted trades.
  • Session ownership information: information indicating which signed-in session is currently allowed to trade in a challenge, so that the same challenge account cannot be traded from two places at once.

Auto Translate

Simora includes an optional auto-translate feature. When it is enabled, the text it translates is sent to Google's public translation endpoint so it can be translated. That text is not sent to Simora's servers and is not stored by Simora.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Simora does not collect or request private keys.
  • Simora does not collect or request seed phrases or recovery phrases.
  • Simora does not connect to, control or use your cryptocurrency wallet.
  • Simora does not execute real blockchain transactions and does not move real funds.
  • Simora does not take custody of cryptocurrency or any other asset.
  • Simora does not send your general browsing history to Simora's servers. The extension only runs on Axiom, GMGN and Padre.
  • Simora does not use your information for advertising or ad targeting.

How We Use Information

Data on your device

  • Simulate paper trades using live market prices.
  • Calculate profit and loss, and update it in real time.
  • Display your open and closed positions and your trading history.
  • Maintain your performance history across sessions.
  • Remember your settings, preferences and interface layout.

Data on Simora's servers

  • Create and maintain your Simora Social profile.
  • Sign you in and keep your session valid, and verify that requests come from your account.
  • Show your profile, and show the profiles you follow.
  • Deliver your shared trade activity to people who follow you, and show you theirs.
  • Let you join and take part in the Weekly Challenge.
  • Keep your challenge balance and progress consistent across the supported platforms you use.
  • Apply Weekly Challenge rules, such as join windows and challenge end times.
  • Calculate challenge statistics such as realized performance, number of trades and win rate.
  • Produce and display the Weekly Challenge leaderboard and your ranking.
  • Keep completed challenge results available after a challenge ends.
  • Make sure a challenge account is only traded from one active session at a time, and prevent duplicate or conflicting participation.

Data Storage

On your device. Your paper trading account, simulated transactions, performance history, limit orders, settings and cached market data are stored using your browser's local storage and the extension's own storage. This data stays in your browser profile. It is not uploaded to Simora, and it is not synchronized between your devices by Simora.

On Simora's servers. The Simora Social and Weekly Challenge information described above is stored on Simora's backend. Simora's backend runs on Cloudflare infrastructure, using Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare's associated storage services. This means Cloudflare processes and stores this information on Simora's behalf.

We apply reasonable technical measures to protect information held on our servers, including authenticated sessions that expire and access controls that tie challenge and profile data to the signed-in account it belongs to. No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we do not claim that it is.

Data Retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it, clear your browser data, or uninstall the extension.

Simora does not currently apply a fixed automatic deletion period to server-side information. Server-side information is retained for as long as necessary to provide Simora Social, Weekly Challenge, leaderboard and related functionality, unless deletion is requested or retention is otherwise required for legitimate operational purposes.

Completed Weekly Challenge results and leaderboard placements are intentionally retained after a challenge ends, so that past results and rankings remain viewable.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not share personal data for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • We do not share your Simora Social or Weekly Challenge information with Axiom, GMGN or Padre.

Information stored on Simora's servers is processed by the service providers we use to operate that infrastructure. Cloudflare provides the hosting and storage for Simora's backend and processes this information on our behalf so that the service can function.

Some information you provide is deliberately shown to other people. This is described in the next section.

We may also disclose information if we are required to do so by law, or where necessary to respond to a valid legal request, to protect our rights, or to investigate abuse of the service.

Public Information

Some Simora features are designed to show information to other people. Please keep this in mind when choosing a username or profile picture.

Weekly Challenge leaderboard. The leaderboard is published publicly on our website and can be viewed by anyone. For ranked participants it shows the username, the profile picture, the rank, the number of completed trades, simulated trading volume, realized return percentage, win rate, realized profit and loss, the number of closed positions, and average holding time. These are simulated results, not real trading results.

Simora Social. If you turn Simora Social on, your username, profile picture and public profile code can be seen by other Simora users, and your completed paper trades are shared with the people who follow your profile. If you turn Simora Social off, new trade activity is no longer shared.

Everything else held on Simora's servers — including your password, session identifiers, installation identifier, the list of profiles you follow, and your individual challenge operation records — is not published and is not shown to other users.

Third-Party Services

Simora works alongside the following supported trading platforms in order to function. Simora reads market information from the page you are on; it does not send your Simora account information to them:

  • Axiom
  • GMGN
  • Padre

Simora also relies on these external services:

  • Cloudflare — hosting and storage for Simora's backend, including Simora Social and the Weekly Challenge.
  • Binance and CoinGecko — public market price data used to value simulated positions. These requests ask for public prices and do not include your personal or account information.
  • Google Translate — used only by the optional auto-translate feature, and only for the text being translated.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and practices. Listing them here describes the technical use Simora makes of them; it does not imply endorsement, and it does not mean they receive your Simora Social or Weekly Challenge data beyond what is described in this policy.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

Online features are optional. You can use Simora's paper trading features without creating a Simora Social profile and without joining the Weekly Challenge. You can turn Simora Social off at any time in the extension.

Data on your device. You can remove locally stored Simora data by clearing the extension's storage, clearing your browser data for the supported platforms, or uninstalling the extension.

Data on Simora's servers. Uninstalling the extension does not by itself delete information already stored on Simora's servers. To ask about, or request deletion of, your Simora Social or Weekly Challenge information, contact simoralabs@gmail.com. Please include your username or public profile code so we can identify the profile. We handle these requests manually and will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Children

Simora is not directed to children and is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to Simora through its online features, please contact us at simoralabs@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.
